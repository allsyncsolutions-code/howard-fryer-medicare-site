How I Can Help You

Whether you're turning 65, retiring, or reviewing your current coverage, I guide you through every step of the Medicare journey.

🛡️

Medicare Advantage (Part C)

All-in-one plans that often include dental, vision, hearing, and prescription drug coverage. I'll compare options in your county.

📄

Medicare Supplement (Medigap)

Plans that help cover deductibles, coinsurance, and copays — giving you predictable costs and freedom to see any doctor who accepts Medicare.

💊

Prescription Drug Plans (Part D)

I check your medications against every available plan to make sure your prescriptions are covered at the lowest possible cost.

🎂

Turning 65 / New to Medicare

From your Initial Enrollment Period to your first plan choice, I'll walk you through the timeline so you never miss a deadline or pay a penalty.

🔄

Annual Plan Reviews

Plans change every year. During AEP (Oct 15 – Dec 7), I'll review your current coverage and switch you if a better fit is available.

👥

Special Enrollment Help

Moving, retiring, losing employer coverage, or qualifying for Extra Help? I'll make sure your transition is seamless.
